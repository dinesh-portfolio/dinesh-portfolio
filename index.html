Are you available for full-time roles?

Yes. I am open to full-time, hybrid, remote and relocation roles in SEO, performance marketing and digital marketing, as well as client projects.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most projects show early movement in 3 to 6 weeks: pages get indexed, impressions grow, easier keywords start to rank, and a Google Business Profile gets more views and calls. Strong rankings for competitive keywords usually take a few months. The pace depends on the type of project, how complete the website is and how competitive the keywords are.

What does your audit cover?

Technical issues, on-page content, keyword gaps against competitors, Google Business Profile, tracking setup and a ranked list of fixes.

Do you work with businesses in other cities?

Yes. My work so far is with brands in Bangalore, Kerala and the US market, and much of it is remote.
